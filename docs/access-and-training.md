# Access & Training

The UCSB Innovation Workshop is a shared fabrication facility available to researchers at the University of California, Santa Barbara. The Workshop also accommodates student project teams, including Capstone, New Ventures, and Bren EcoE programs, as well as approved users from outside UCSB.

Access to the Innovation Workshop requires completion of the following steps **in order**:

1. [Facility Access Request](#1-facility-access-request)
2. [New User Orientation](#2-new-user-orientation)
3. [UC Laboratory Safety Training](#3-uc-laboratory-safety-training)
4. [Card-Key Access](#4-card-key-access)

---

## Who Can Use the Innovation Workshop?

The Innovation Workshop is available to: 
- UCSB undergraduates, graduate students, post docs, professors, and other university staff working on UC-funded projects
- Companies that want to use the Innovation Workshop for prototyping or testing new equipment
- Elementary, middle school, and high school groups for tours and maker activities

If you are unsure if the Innovation Workshop is the right place for your work, contact the lab manager Brian Dincau (bdincau@ucsb.edu)



---

## Getting Access to the Innovation Workshop

New users must complete the following four steps before receiving card-key access to the Workshop.

### 1. Facility Access Request

Before using **any CNSI shared facility**, you must submit the initial **Facility Access Request** through the CNSI.

[**Submit the Facility Access Request**](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/0bf7aeaf40e741f4b463e536b2282e53)

### CCOA Accounting String

Academic users must have their **CCOA accounting string** available when completing the Facility Access Request.

Your CCOA accounting string must be verified before you can proceed with Workshop training.

---

### 2. New User Orientation

All new users must complete the **Innovation Workshop New User Orientation** before receiving card-key access to the Workshop.

The orientation introduces users to the Workshop's facilities, general safety requirements, access procedures, and expectations for working in the labs.

After submitting your Facility Access Request, use the Training Request Form to request:

- New User Orientation
- Training on specific Workshop tools

[**Request New User Orientation or Tool Training**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSewzNVlJc5TImlY7OTR7UUEUHzaQ3CSZ5R6joGe0HDVbSZMXw/viewform)

> **Important:** Submit your Facility Access Request before requesting New User Orientation or tool training.

---

### 3. UC Laboratory Safety Training

The University of California requires everyone working in a laboratory to complete the online **UC Laboratory Safety Fundamentals** training.

The initial training must be completed before card-key access can be approved. A refresher is required annually.

#### How to Complete the Training

1. Go to the [UCSB Learning Center](https://learningcenter.ucsb.edu/) and log in.
2. Search for **Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety**.
3. Complete the online course.
4. If you have already completed the initial course, complete the required annual refresher if prompted.
5. Send a copy of your completion certificate to the Workshop Manager.

### Undergraduate Users

If you are an undergraduate and cannot log in to the UCSB Learning Center, contact your **PI or advisor**. They can request that the system send you an invitation to enroll in the required training.

---

### 4. Card-Key Access

After completing the Facility Access Request, New User Orientation, and required Laboratory Safety Training, you can request card-key access to the Innovation Workshop.

The card-key access form is only available when connected to the **UCSB campus network or VPN**.

[**Request Card-Key Access**](https://access.cnsi.ucsb.edu/)

### Workshop Rooms

The Innovation Workshop occupies two rooms in Elings Hall:

- **Elings 3430**
- **Elings 2442**

When requesting access, be sure to request access to **both Workshop rooms** as well as the appropriate **exterior doors**.

> **Tip:** If you want your Workshop access approved as quickly as possible, request only the Workshop rooms and exterior doors. Submit a separate card-key request for access to other CNSI rooms.
