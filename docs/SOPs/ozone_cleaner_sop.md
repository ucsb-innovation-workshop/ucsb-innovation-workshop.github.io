# Ozone Cleaner Training SOP

## Location

The ozone cleaner is located inside the fume hood closest to the Objet printer in the Microfluidics Lab, Elings Hall 3430.

## Safety

- Check with lab staff before using the chamber for anything other than bonding PDMS to glass.
- Make sure the chamber is sealed before use.
- **Ozone is a health hazard.** Use the cleaner inside the fume hood.
- Never disassemble or modify the unit.
- Check substrate height before closing the chamber to avoid damaging the UV bulb.

## Operating Procedures

1. Place the substrates on the platform inside the chamber.
2. Check substrate height using the green tape on the ruler. **Do not skip this step.** The UV bulb is easily broken, and the original SOP lists its replacement cost as $3,000.
3. Close the chamber lid.
4. Turn on the UV lamps by pressing the **Start** switch.
5. Wait for the **10-minute cleaning cycle** to finish.
6. Leave the substrates in the closed chamber for an **additional 1–2 minutes** after cleaning, as specified on the tool page.
7. Open the chamber and remove the substrates.
8. Clean the chamber platform if necessary.
