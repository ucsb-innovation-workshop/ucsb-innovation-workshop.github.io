## Safety Concerns

- Wear safety glasses, closed-toe shoes, and long pants when operating the drill press.
- Metal chips can be sharp. Remove them with an acid brush; do not touch them directly.
- Avoid inhaling fumes produced when drilling with oil.
- Do **not** wear gloves while operating the machine.
- Wait until the machine stops completely before touching the tool or spindle.

---

## Typical Use Cases

The WEN vertical drill press is used for drilling holes in workpieces. Its speed control lever is on the left side of the head, and the vertical quill feed is on the right. Return the chuck key to its holder on the right side of the head after use.

---

## Specifications

- **Spindle speed range:** 280–3,100 RPM (740–3,100 RPM on the main pulley)
- **Quill extension:** 4⅜ in.
- **Maximum drill bit diameter:** 16 mm
- **Maximum workpiece height:** 44 in.
