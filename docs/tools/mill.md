## Safety Concerns

- Untrained or unsupervised operators risk serious injury.
- Flying chips can cause metal splinters and burns. Wear safety goggles, closed-toe shoes, and long pants.
- Keep body parts clear of the spindle and workpiece while the machine is moving.
- Use care when handling heavy objects.
- Secure the workpiece and cutting tool before starting a machining operation.
- Remove chuck keys and other tools before starting the machine.

---

## Typical Use Cases

This benchtop vertical milling machine is used to manually machine small parts from metal, wood, and plastic.

It falls between the Haas CNC mill and the ShopBot desktop router in capability. Because it is less rigid than larger milling machines, select the depth of cut carefully.

---

## Specifications

- **Table size:** 19 × 7 × 13 in.
- **Spindle taper:** R8
- **Spindle speed range:** 50–2,500 RPM
- **Motor power:** 1 HP
- **Vise gripping range:** 4.5 × 4 in.
