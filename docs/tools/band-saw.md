## About

This vertical bandsaw is one of the primary cutting tools in the Innovation Workshop. Capable of resawing large blocks of wood or plastic or cutting through softer metals, this bandsaw turns perfectly good raw material into scrap faster than any other tool.

Blades can be swapped out with the help of a Workshop Wizard to better suit the material being cut, and the speed is infinitely adjustable to tune in the perfect cut.

---

## Safety Concerns

This saw is incredibly powerful, and fingers/hands should be kept far away from the blade when in operation. Use a sled, push rod, and fence whenever possible, and make sure the blade TPI is matched to the thickness and type of material.

**RUNNING THE BLADE TOO FAST WILL SHEAR THE TEETH RIGHT OFF THE BLADE.**

If you do not know the correct feeds and speeds, please ask a Workshop Wizard.
