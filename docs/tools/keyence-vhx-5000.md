## About

The Keyence VHX-5000 is a semi-computer-controlled microscope capable of taking precision measurements from large-depth-of-field images under high magnification. The microscope features both backlighting and top lighting, as well as a computer-controlled movable stage that can be used to pan across the object being viewed.

The motorized objective can be used to image parts with a large depth of field and create a single in-focus image with depth-mapping data. The motorized stage also allows for the stitching of several images together to create larger, high-magnification pictures.

---

## Safety Concerns

- Do not leave lighting elements on when the microscope is not in use.
- Do not crash the objectives into the sample or stage.
- If replacing the bulb in the MI-150, wear gloves to avoid getting oil on the halogen lamp bulb.

## Detailed Specifications

- Z travel: 17 mm/s
- Objective rotation: Up to 90° from the normal stage axis
- Image stitching: Up to 20,000 × 20,000 pixels
- Optical zoom: 0× to 1000×
- Maximum frame rate: 50 FPS
