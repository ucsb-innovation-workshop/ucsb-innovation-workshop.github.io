**Tool Type:** Observation / Measurement / Imaging
**Manufacturer:** Keyence
**Location:** Elings 3430

## Safety Concerns

* Do not leave lighting elements on when the microscope is not in use.
* Do not crash the objectives into the sample or stage.
* If replacing the bulb in the MI-150, wear gloves to avoid getting oil on the halogen lamp bulb.

## Typical Use Cases

* High-magnification imaging
* Precision measurements
* Large-depth-of-field imaging
* Image stitching
* 3D depth-mapping
* Surface inspection

## Specifications

* **Z travel:** 17 mm/s
* **Objective rotation:** Up to 90° from the normal stage axis
* **Image stitching:** Up to 20,000 × 20,000 pixels
* **Optical zoom:** 0× to 1000×
* **Maximum frame rate:** 50 FPS
