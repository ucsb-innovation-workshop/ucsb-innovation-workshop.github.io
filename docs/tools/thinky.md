## About

The Thinky Mixer is one of the PDMS tools in the Microfluidics Station in Elings 3430. This machine mixes and de-aerates elastomers used in the fabrication of microfluidic devices. Mixing time and revolution speed can be programmed using the machine’s controller interface.

---

## Safety Concerns

- Wear goggles, gloves, a lab coat, long pants, and closed-toe shoes while operating this machine.
- Tie long hair back.
- Review the Sylgard 184 resin SOP before handling the material.

## Detailed Specifications

- **Capacity:** 310 g
- **Centrifugal Force:** Over 400 G
- **Adapter:** Cold-insulated and heat-resistant to support various material characteristics
- **Control Functions:** Memory and step-operation functions for controlling and executing operating conditions
- **Operating Modes:** Mixing and deaeration modes enable rapid dispersion and deaeration of high-viscosity materials
