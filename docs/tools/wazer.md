## Safety Concerns  
- Always wear safety glasses when using the machine.
- Wear hearing protection if staying near the machine for long periods of time.
- Wear gloves when handling material, as cutting may result in sharp edges.
- Remove leftover material from previous jobs before running a new job.
- Never leave the waterjet unattended while it is running.
- Disconnect power and turn off the water supply before leaving the waterjet unattended.


## Typical Use Cases  
The Wazer Desktop Waterjet Cutter uses a powerful stream of water and abrasive to cut materials of all sorts. Always check materials list BEFORE attempting to cut/engrave a material. It utilizes .dxf or .svg files that are uploaded via SD card to the machine to determine the toolpaths.


## Specifications  
- **Cutting area (W × D):** 12 × 18 in
- **Max. height of workpiece:** Varies by material; check the [Materials Specs](https://wazer.com/materials/materials-specs/#materials-table).
- **Loading area (W × D):** 13 × 19 in
- **Overall dimensions (W × D × H):** 34 × 26 × 22 in
- **Max. gantry speed:** 59 ipm
- **Maximum water flow rate (@ 4000 psi):** 0.5 gallons/min


**Power consumption:**
  - **Waterjet Main Unit:** 100–240 VAC, 50/60 Hz, 2 A, 200 W
  - **Pump Box:** 110–120 VAC, 60 Hz, 14 A, 1,500 W
