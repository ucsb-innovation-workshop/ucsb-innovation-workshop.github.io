## Safety Concerns

- Trimethylchlorosilane is flammable, toxic, and reacts with water vapor. It can cause illness if inhaled and may ignite when reacting with air or water vapor.
- Use trimethylchlorosilane only in the vapor silanization rig inside a fume hood.
- Do not let the pressure inside the chamber exceed 15 in Hg.
- Open the silane valve only in a nitrogen atmosphere.
- Wear protective eyewear, a lab coat, and gloves.
- Keep the fume hood closed when you are not working inside it.
- Consult the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) before handling the silane.

## Trimethylchlorosilane

**Synonyms:**

- TMCS
- Trimethylchlorosilane
- Trimethylsilyl chloride

**Formula:** C₃H₉ClSi  
**Molecular Weight:** 108.64 g/mol  
**CAS Number:** 75-77-4  
**EC Number:** 200-900-5  

This protocol is intended for gas-phase monolayer deposition of a silane to serve as a counter-adhesion agent for glass or silicon substrates. Trimethylchlorosilane is referred to as “silane” throughout the protocol. It can be deposited onto silicon and glass substrates and can etch metal oxides, such as alumina.
