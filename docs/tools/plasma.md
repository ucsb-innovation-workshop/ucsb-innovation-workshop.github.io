## About

The Harrick PDC-32G is a compact tabletop plasma cleaner for nanoscale surface cleaning and activation of small samples. The attached PlasmaFlo gas mixer allows quantitative control of up to two different process gases and monitoring of the vacuum pressure within the chamber.

This tool is plumbed for nitrogen plasma only, which works well for PDMS-to-glass and PDMS-to-PDMS bonding. If you require oxygen plasma, consider using the [plasma chamber in the Nanostructures Cleanroom Facility](https://www.cnsi.ucsb.edu/facilities/equipment/yes-r3-plasma-cleaning-system).

---

## Safety Concerns

- The plasma chamber uses **RF radiation**.
- If you have a pacemaker or similar device, consult your doctor before using this tool.

## Recipes

Brian Dincau found that the following recipe works well for bonding PDMS to PDMS or glass:

- **Power:** High
- **Pressure:** 440–460 mTorr
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Nitrogen Flow:** Adjust as needed to maintain the desired pressure.
- **After Treatment:** Mate the surfaces immediately, then transfer to an oven at 115°C for 20 minutes.
