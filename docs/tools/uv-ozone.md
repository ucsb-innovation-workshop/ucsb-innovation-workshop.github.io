## Safety Concerns

- **Ultraviolet Radiation Hazard:** A device interlock keeps the UV bulb off when the chamber is open.
- **Pinching Hazard:** Keep fingers clear when closing the lid.
- **Electrical Hazard:** No liquids are allowed.
- **Gas Exposure:** Leave the sample in the chamber for an additional **1–2 minutes** after cleaning.
- **Sample Height:** Keep the sample below the height of the clearance block to avoid damaging the tool. Place the block on the metal base, below the sample platform.

## Typical Use Cases

- Removing organic contaminants from sample surfaces.
- Non-acidic, dry surface cleaning using ultraviolet light and ozone.

The cleaner uses 185 nm and 254 nm ultraviolet light. In the presence of oxygen, the 185 nm light produces ozone, while the 254 nm light excites organic molecules on the surface. Together, these processes break down organic contaminants.

## Specifications
