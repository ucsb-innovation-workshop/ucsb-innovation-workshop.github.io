## About

This tool is a small lathe capable of manually machining small parts out of metals, wood, and plastic. Standard operations include facing, turning, drilling, and cutting workpieces with axial symmetry.

---

## Safety Concerns

- This machine is designed for operation by experienced users familiar with metalworking hazards.
- Wear safety glasses or face protection when operating this tool.
- Wear closed-toe shoes and long pants during use, and tie long hair back.
- Ensure that both the tool and the workpiece are securely fastened before starting an operation.

## Detailed Specifications

- **Distance Between Centers:** 30 in.
- **Spindle Speeds:** Infinitely variable, 75–2,000 RPM (2 steps)
- **Cross Slide Travel:** 6 in.
- **Compound Travel:** 3 in.
- **Tailstock Quill Travel:** 4 in.
- **Main Motor:** 1.5 HP, variable speed
