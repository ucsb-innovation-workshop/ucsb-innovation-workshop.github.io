    CNSI Innovation Workshop and Microfluidics Lab Laboratory Ovens Operating Procedures

    Location

    Oven Location
    Thermoline / Lindberg Blue M Oven Room 2448
    Yamato DKN400 Convection Oven Room 3430
    Lindberg Blue M Convection Oven Room 3430

    Overview

    This training introduces the use and operation of the laboratory ovens, including:

    • Personal protective equipment (PPE)
    • Operational safety checks
    • Operating instructions
    • Oven specifications

    Personal Protective Equipment

    • Wear safety glasses, long pants, and closed-toe shoes.
    • Wear thermally insulating gloves when handling hot items. Gloves are usually located on the bench next to the ovens.

    Operational Safety Checks

    1. Familiarize yourself with the oven's operation and controls.
    2. Do not place food in laboratory ovens.
    3. Never heat a sealed container.
    4. Never heat flammable or combustible materials in an oven. A fire or explosion may result.
    5. Do not heat materials that may pose an acute or chronic toxicity hazard unless an SOP has been prepared with laboratory staff to safely vent the oven.
    6. Do not operate an oven if it is damaged or malfunctioning. Place a note on the oven to alert other users and notify laboratory staff.
    7. Use tongs or thermally insulating gloves to handle hot items. Be aware of nearby people when placing or removing items.
    8. Secondary containment is required when heating containers of liquid to prevent leaks from contaminating the oven. This is especially important when heating PDMS molds.

    Operating Instructions

    1. Enter the desired temperature set point using the appropriate controller instructions below.
    2. Place your items in the oven. Place liquid-containing materials on a baking sheet or other secondary container.
    3. Set the countdown timer for the length of your run. If the timer reads zero, another user who needs the oven may remove your items.
    4. When finished, remove your items and close the door.
    5. Follow the power instructions for the oven's location:
    6. Room 3430: Ovens are usually left powered on.
    7. Room 2448: The oven is usually turned off after each run but may be left on if repeated runs are planned.

    Maximum Temperatures

    Oven Maximum Temperature
    Yamato DKN400 Convection Oven 260 °C
    Lindberg Blue M Convection Oven 300 °C
    Thermoline / Lindberg Blue M Oven Not specified in the original SOP; confirm with laboratory staff before use

    Changing the Temperature Set Point

    Blue M Ovens

    1. Use the ↑ / ↓ buttons to adjust the temperature.
    2. Press SET/ENT to confirm.

    Yamato Oven

    1. Press FIXED TEMP.
    2. Use the ↑ / ↓ buttons to adjust the temperature.
    3. Press SET/ENT to confirm.

    Setting the Countdown Timers

    Ovens in Room 3430

    1. Press 1.
    2. Enter the run time using the H / M / S buttons.
    3. Press 1 to start the countdown.
    4. Press and hold 1 to reset the timer.

    Oven in Room 2448

    1. Make sure the slide switch on the right is set to Countdown.
    2. Enter the run time using the H / M / S buttons.
    3. Press Start/Stop to start the countdown.
